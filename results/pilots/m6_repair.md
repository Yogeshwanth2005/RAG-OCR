# Pilot M6 stage 1: rule-based letter repair

Run: `python pilots/m6_repair_eval.py` from the repository root. Generated by the script; numbers are from that run.

## Setup

- Repair: `ocrrag.repair.repair_text`. Rules in order: `rejoin_mark`, `dedupe_mark`, `rejoin_conjunct` (Telugu only), `nfc`.
- Metric: `ocrrag.align.error_rate`, akshara units. Real OCR is scored with `ocrrag.text.strip_invisible` on both sides ("scored"); the repaired OCR is stripped after repair.
- Synthetic: first 300 non-empty lines of each sample file, injected with `inject_akshara_errors` at word error rate 0.10, op weights {'split_mark': 1.0, 'virama_drop': 1.0}, seeds 0-9 (a fresh `random.Random(seed)` per seed, applied per sentence). Error rate is measured against the clean sentence.
- Fixed (synthetic): a sentence counts as fixed when `normalize(repaired) == normalize(clean)`. Two shares are reported. The **pure** share uses only sentences whose sole injected op is that op; it is the headline. The **all-sentences** share uses sentences with exactly one entry of that op. It counts sentences that also had another injected op as unfixed, because those cannot match the clean sentence through this repair, so that share is biased down.
- Real OCR: Tesseract outputs from P1 (`data/pilots/p1_ocr/<page_id>_L<level>.txt`) against `<page_id>_gt.txt`. Figures are the mean over the 20 pages of the language. A page-level result is one page at one level (80 per language, 160 in total).
- Over-correction: `repair_text` on every non-empty line of the full clean sample files. Changed words are counted with a token diff.
- Runtime: 197.9 s.

## Step 3: decision

- Scope: Tesseract output from P1 only (160 page-level results: 2 languages x 20 pages x 4 levels). No VLM or other OCR engine was tested.
- Synthetic, pure-sentence headline: split_mark fixed 100.0% (n=1992) in Telugu and 100.0% (n=1688) in Tamil; virama_drop fixed 0.0% (n=620) in Telugu and 0.0% (n=838) in Tamil. Sentence AER falls from 0.0662 to 0.0125 (te) and from 0.0421 to 0.0070 (ta).
- Real OCR effect: 2 page-level gain(s) and 1 page-level loss(es) out of 160; largest gain 0.00089 AER (te_p10 L0), largest loss 0.00087 AER (te_p12 L2). Net mean change -0.000006 AER per page-level result.
- Rules fired 5 times across the 160 real-OCR results (dedupe_mark=2; rejoin_mark=3).
- Whitespace directly before a mark (the pattern `rejoin_mark` repairs): GT 0 occurrences across 40 GT pages; OCR before repair 3 across 160 OCR outputs (te 2, ta 1).
- Residual real-OCR error types after repair, Telugu (all levels): deletion 17453, word_merge 4910, consonant 343, matra 243, other 133.
- Residual real-OCR error types after repair, Tamil (all levels): deletion 6911, word_merge 1362, other 370, virama 256, consonant 160.
- Over-correction: 0 changed words across 4457 clean lines (0 lines changed). No rule fired on clean text (none), so this is a no-false-positive check on clean text only.
- Verdict (scoped to Tesseract output in this pilot): negligible effect (2 gain(s), 1 loss(es), net -0.000006 AER per result). Keep the rules as an optional, safe stage (0 changed clean words), and re-test on VLM and other OCR engines in Plan 2, whose errors may include detached marks.
- Error types the rules cannot reach (targets for M6 stage 2 in Plan 4): virama_drop (pure fixed share 0.0% (n=620) te, 0.0% (n=838) ta; no rule restores a dropped virama); matra_swap, consonant_swap and other substitutions; deletions of non-space units (content lost by Tesseract, the bulk of L2-L3 error); word merges (a space deleted between two words); and conjunct errors apart from Telugu stray-space conjuncts.
