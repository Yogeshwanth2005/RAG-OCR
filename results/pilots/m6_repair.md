# Pilot M6 stage 1: rule-based letter repair

Run: `python pilots/m6_repair_eval.py` from the repository root. Generated by the script; numbers are from that run.

## Setup

- Repair: `ocrrag.repair.repair_text`. Rules in order: `rejoin_mark`, `dedupe_mark`, `rejoin_conjunct` (Telugu only), `nfc`.
- Metric: `ocrrag.align.error_rate`, akshara units. Real OCR is scored with `ocrrag.text.strip_invisible` on both sides ("scored"); the repaired OCR is stripped after repair.
- Synthetic: first 300 non-empty lines of each sample file, injected with `inject_akshara_errors` at word error rate 0.10, op weights {'split_mark': 1.0, 'virama_drop': 1.0}, seeds 0-9 (a fresh `random.Random(seed)` per seed, applied per sentence). Error rate is measured against the clean sentence.
- Fixed (synthetic): per sentence with exactly one injected entry of that op, fixed means `normalize(repaired) == normalize(clean)`. The count of such sentences that also contained other injected ops is shown, because those sentences cannot become exact matches through an op the rules do not reach.
- Real OCR: Tesseract outputs from P1 (`data/pilots/p1_ocr/<page_id>_L<level>.txt`) against `<page_id>_gt.txt`. Figures are the mean over the 20 pages of the language.
- Over-correction: `repair_text` on every non-empty line of the full clean sample files. Changed words are counted with a token diff.
- Runtime: 202.5 s.

## Step 3: decision

- Synthetic: split_mark is fixed in 91.6% (te) and 81.7% (ta) of single-error sentences; virama_drop in 0.0% (te) and 0.0% (ta). Sentence AER falls from 0.0662 to 0.0125 (te) and from 0.0421 to 0.0070 (ta).
- Real OCR, Telugu at L3: AER 0.6238 -> 0.6238 (change +0.00000). Pages worse after repair across all levels: 1.
- Real OCR, Tamil at L3: AER 0.3279 -> 0.3279 (change +0.00000). Pages worse after repair across all levels: 0.
- Over-correction: 0 changed words across 4457 clean lines (0 lines changed).
- Verdict: do not keep rule repair as a stage before indexing. It is safe on clean text (0 changed words) but it gains nothing measurable on real OCR: the largest mean change in any language and level is 0.00004 AER, the rules fired 5 times across 160 page-level results, and 1 of them got worse. The synthetic gain does not carry over, because Tesseract output almost never puts a space before a mark (the pattern `rejoin_mark` needs). Its main real boundary error is the reverse: spaces deleted, so words merge.
- Error types the rules cannot reach (targets for M6 stage 2 in Plan 4): virama_drop (synthetic fixed share 0.000 in both languages; no rule restores a dropped virama); matra_swap, consonant_swap and other substitutions; deletions of non-space units (content lost by Tesseract, the bulk of L2-L3 error); word merges (a space deleted between two words); and conjunct errors apart from Telugu stray-space conjuncts.
- Residual real-OCR error types after repair, Telugu (all levels): deletion 17453, word_merge 4910, consonant 343, matra 243, other 133.
- Residual real-OCR error types after repair, Tamil (all levels): deletion 6911, word_merge 1362, other 370, virama 256, consonant 160.
