# Pilot P1 and P8: rendering, Tesseract error profile, confidence calibration

Run: `python pilots/p1_render_ocr.py` from the repository root. Generated by the script; numbers are from that run.

## Setup

- Text: `data/samples/{te,ta}_sample.txt` (Wikipedia, CC BY-SA, see `data/samples/SOURCES.md`). Sentences are taken in file order, one per line; 5 sentences form a paragraph; sentences are added until a page reaches about 250 words.
- Pages: 20 per language, `te_p01`..`te_p20` and `ta_p01`..`ta_p20`. Fonts alternate Sans (odd index) and Serif (even index). Pages 1-10 are `one_col`, pages 11-20 are `two_col`. Rendered with `ocrrag.render` at 150 dpi, A4, 12 pt.
- Overflow: 72 sentence(s) dropped in total after the renderer's overflow error; each dropped sentence starts the next page. Pages with drops: ta_p01 (2), ta_p02 (4), ta_p03 (1), ta_p04 (2), ta_p05 (2), ta_p06 (4), ta_p07 (2), ta_p08 (3), ta_p09 (1), ta_p10 (2), ta_p11 (6), ta_p12 (6), ta_p13 (3), ta_p14 (5), ta_p15 (6), ta_p16 (5), ta_p17 (2), ta_p18 (4), ta_p19 (3), ta_p20 (9).
- Degradation: `ocrrag.degrade.degrade(png, level, seed=page index)`, levels 0-3:
  - level 0: unchanged
  - level 1: Gaussian blur + Gaussian noise
  - level 2: level 1 + downscale to 60% + skew up to 1 degree
  - level 3: level 2 + ink bleed + scanner line artefacts
- OCR: Tesseract 5.3.4 via `ocrrag.ocr_tesseract` (`tel` for Telugu, `tam` for Tamil; `--psm 3`).
- Two scorings of every image:
  - **raw**: the text as the renderer and Tesseract produced it.
  - **scored**: `ocrrag.text.strip_invisible` (removes U+200C and U+200D) applied to both ground truth and OCR, then the same metrics. Tesseract emits word-final ZWNJ in Tamil that the ground truth does not contain; in raw scoring these count as errors. Telugu is affected much less.
  - The level tables show both. The error-type tables, the confusion models (`p1_confusions_*.json`), P8 and the per-image table use **scored** text.
- Metrics (`ocrrag.align.error_rate`, NFC-normalised, whitespace collapsed): CER (char units), AER (akshara units), WER (word units). Each level figure is the mean over the 20 pages of that language.
- Mean conf: mean of the per-page mean Tesseract word confidence (0-100), over pages with at least one OCR word. Pages with zero OCR words are counted separately.
- Error types (scored): one op per non-equal akshara alignment step. Substitutions get `ocrrag.align.classify`'s label. Insertions and deletions of non-space units are counted as **insertion** and **deletion**. Space insertions and deletions keep `classify`'s **boundary**. **other** therefore means a substitution `classify` could not label.
- Per-image values: `results/pilots/p1_per_image.csv` (80 rows per language per run: 20 pages x 4 levels). P8 is computed from this file.
- Ground truth and OCR text: `data/pilots/p1_ocr/<page_id>_gt.txt`, `<page_id>_L<level>.txt` (git-ignored).

Controller operationalisation: PASS if Spearman rho <= -0.5 and p < 0.01; otherwise FAIL. Unit of analysis: one image (page at one level), x = page mean word confidence, y = page AER (scored). Read from `p1_per_image.csv`.
